# The Quiet Kingdom · ငြိမ်သက်သော နိုင်ငံတော်

A chill, relaxing 3D exploring and learning game for kids aged 8 to 12, in Burmese and English.

**▶ Play:** https://brucekhant.github.io/quiet-kingdom-game/

- Two maps to explore: the Quiet Kingdom 🏰 and Jungle Island 🌴 🌋
- 💎 Wisdom stones with math, animal, English and "did you know?" questions
- Rewards: fly 🪽, super speed ⚡, super jump 🦘, giant 🦖, tiny 🐭, treasure compass 🧭 and more
- Levels from 🌱 Beginner to 👑 Legend, and 24 treasures to find
- Put your own face and name on the character (the photo stays on your device)
- Lofi music, day and night, and it works on phones and computers

Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari. On a phone, use **Add to Home Screen** to play it like an app.
